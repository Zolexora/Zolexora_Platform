# Zolexora Platform

Zolexora_Platform is the monorepo containing the complete Zolexora Platform ecosystem.

## Architecture Boundaries

* **Platform Core** is located at: `platform/`
* **ZolexoraERP** is located at: `products/erp/`
* **Products** are located at: `products/`
* **Shared packages** are located at: `packages/`
* **Clients** are located at: `clients/`
* **Infrastructure** is located at: `infrastructure/`

The objective is: ONE MONOREPO, ONE CONNECTED ECOSYSTEM, CLEAR PRODUCT BOUNDARIES.
