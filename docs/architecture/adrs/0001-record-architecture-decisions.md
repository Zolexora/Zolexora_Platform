# 1. Record Architecture Decisions

Date: 2026-10-09

## Status
Accepted

## Context
We need to record the architectural decisions made on this project. Without a standardized way of recording decisions, future team members will struggle to understand *why* certain technologies (like Frappe vs FastAPI) or architectures were chosen.

## Decision
We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs). 

## Consequences
We will have a log of decisions. Each record will describe the context, the decision, and its consequences.
